**Working title:** *The Scottish Courtroom in a Dutch Forest*
*(Alternatives: "The Day Scotland Moved to the Netherlands" · "Lockerbie: A Verdict, Not an Ending")*
**Runtime:** 11:10 · 3840×2160 · 24 fps
**Hook:** A fence in the Dutch woods that Dutch police can't cross, and a Scottish court behind it.
**Turn:** At the midpoint the verdict appears to end the story. Then the judges' own words reopen it, and the case comes down to whether some Christmas lights were on.

## Why Zeist, and why now

Zeist is the only place anyone has ever stood trial for Lockerbie (so far), so the location *is* the hook. Most Lockerbie videos open on the bombing. This one opens on a courtroom that shouldn't exist.

The timing is live. The US trial of a third accused man, Abu Agila Mas'ud, is due in **January 2027**. A video published before jury selection rides the news cycle. The cost is that **Act VIII has to be checked against the docket on recording day and on publish day.** See `SOURCES.md` → *Before you publish*.

## Start here

1. Read `PIPELINE-krita-blender.md` → **Ground rules** first. No victims on screen, no explosion, sketch likenesses not caricatures, and no logos. Every other decision follows from these.
2. Read `SOURCES.md`, then read the 2001 Opinion of the Court yourself. Both on-screen quotes come from it.
3. Record a scratch VO and cut it to time. The script is written to 139 wpm. Acts III and VII run within about 3 s of their slots, so if your read is slower, trim there first.
4. Paint the eight Krita style frames. **Don't open Blender until they're done.**
5. Build the animatic. The radar shot (`02C`) and the toggle shot (`07C`) are the two images the film depends on. If they don't work in the animatic, they won't work rendered.
