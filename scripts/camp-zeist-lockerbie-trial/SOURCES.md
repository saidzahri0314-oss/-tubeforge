# FACT CHECK — The Lockerbie trial at Camp Zeist

All 70 claims in `SCRIPT.md` (VO and on-screen text), graded in script order. **Verify the ⚠️ and ❌ rows against a primary source before publishing.** This is a real mass murder with living families, an acquitted defendant who is still alive, and a defendant awaiting trial. The tolerance for error is lower than for *Dancing Plague*.

> **How this draft was researched.** The table was built in October 2026 from web-search summaries of the sources listed below. The primary documents (the 2001 judgment, the treaty, the SCCRC summaries) were **not** read in full for this draft. Reading the 2001 Opinion of the Court yourself is the single most valuable fact-check step left.

## Before you publish

1. **Re-check Act VIII on the day you record and on the day you publish.** Late-August and September 2026 reports disagree on the new start date (11 January vs 19 January 2027). The script says only "January 2027" on purpose. If the trial has started or ended, rewrite Act VIII and the last line of the Close. Fill in the struck-through dates on the `09D` plate from the docket, not from memory.
2. **Mas'ud is presumed innocent.** VO and captions must keep *accuse*, *allegedly*, *pleaded not guilty* and the defence's duress position. If the channel has a UK audience (it does), also check whether any **Scottish** proceedings against him are "active" under the Contempt of Court Act 1981. If they are, get a media lawyer's read before publishing.
3. **Fhimah was acquitted.** Nothing in the picture, the VO, the thumbnail or the title may suggest otherwise.
4. Check the two judgment quotes (rows 44 and 51) against the Opinion of the Court, character for character.
5. Expect YouTube to treat terrorism as a sensitive topic for ads. The film shows no violence, which is the best case, but self-certify accurately.

## Editorial position

**The script doesn't say whether Megrahi was guilty.** It gives the strongest documented fact on each side in the order events happened: the verdict, the judges' own caveat (verbatim), the 2007 SCCRC finding, Libya's acceptance of responsibility and its prime minister's "buy peace" remark, the families' split, and the 2021 appeal court upholding the conviction. A viewer should come away unsure which side the narrator is on, because the record genuinely supports that uncertainty.

The credibility beat is the **"Scottish soil" correction** in Act IV. It mirrors the "fifteen a day" debunk in *Dancing Plague*. Correcting a claim the audience has already heard, on camera, is what earns the right to walk them through the Act VI evidence without sounding like a conspiracy channel.

**Not claimed anywhere in the script:**
- that Megrahi was innocent, or that he was guilty beyond what the courts found;
- that the timer fragment was planted or fabricated. No court has accepted that, and the SCCRC declined to refer on fragment-related grounds;
- that Iran, Syria or the PFLP-GC carried out the bombing. At trial the defence lodged a special defence of incrimination pointing at Palestinian groups, and the court wasn't persuaded. That thread was **cut deliberately**: it needs three minutes on its own, and it's where the comment section will go. It's a natural follow-up video;
- any reward amount;
- that Mas'ud built the bomb;
- a precise witness count;
- the phrase "resembles him a lot." It's widely quoted, but sources conflict on whether Gauci said it about Megrahi in court or about a photograph of someone else years earlier. The script uses the judgment's own words instead (row 51).
