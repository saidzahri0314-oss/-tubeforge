# PIP WANTS TO FLY

**Format:** 30-second animated kids' Short · 9:16 vertical · stylized 3D cartoon (preschool look)
**Audience:** ages 3–7, and the grown-ups watching with them
**Logline:** A tiny penguin tries three silly ways to fly — flapping, sliding, balloons — splashes into the sea, and discovers that penguins *do* fly: underwater.
**Takeaway:** Penguins use their wings to "fly" through the water. (And your way of flying might just be different.)
**Shots:** 13 · **VO:** optional, 41 words · **Loop:** the last frame is the first frame

> Prompts: [`PROMPTS-higgsfield.md`](PROMPTS-higgsfield.md) · Settings: [`SETTINGS-higgsfield.md`](SETTINGS-higgsfield.md)

## Why it holds attention

| Device | Where |
|---|---|
| Motion on frame 1 and a question in the first second | 0:00 — Pip mid-flap, "Can a penguin fly?" |
| Rule of three, each try bigger than the last | flap (an inch off the ice) → slide (across the slope) → balloons (into the sky) |
| A fresh color world as each act starts | snow white 0:00 → sky blue 0:12 → turquoise sea 0:19 → back to the shore 0:25.5 |
| A visual + sound hit every 2–3 s | flap, whoosh, plop, zip, fwump, wheee, achoo, splash, zoom, ta-da |
| The twist lands before attention dips | 0:19–0:21 (63–70% in) — the answer to the opening question |
| The payoff is a real fact | penguins "fly" underwater — parents like it, kids repeat it |
| Seamless loop | shot 13 ends on shot 01's first frame; Shorts replay automatically |
| No dialogue needed | reads with the sound off and in any language — the VO is optional |

## Characters & world

**Pip** — a little round penguin: navy-black back, cream belly and heart-shaped face patch, big dark-brown sparkly eyes, tiny orange beak and feet, a curly three-feather tuft, and a chunky cherry-red knitted scarf with one white stripe. Determined, bouncy, never gives up. Pip emotes and makes little sounds but never talks on screen — no lip-sync to go wrong.

**Seabirds** — three small round white birds with grey wingtips. Background only.
**Fish** — a few round, friendly fish in sunny yellow, coral pink and lime green.
**Snowball Point** — a sunny polar shore: a low ice ledge over turquoise water, a snowy slope ending in a small snow ramp, a big fluffy snowdrift, rounded icebergs, puffy clouds.
**Under the Ice** — bright aqua water, god rays, floating ice chunks overhead, bubbles.

**Continuity:** the sun is always upper-left · Pip's progress always moves left → right · always five balloons, always the same five colors.

## VO script (optional)

41 words: warm, excited storyteller, quick bouncy pace, with room left for the sound effects. Hit **ONE / TWO / THREE** and **IS** hard. The timestamps are where each line starts.

```text

[0:02.6] Pip wants to try!
[0:04.6] One… flap, flap… plop!
[0:07.1] Two… slide…
[0:09.6] …whoosh… oops!
[0:12.1] Three… balloons! He's flying!
[0:15.3] Uh-oh…
[0:19.4] Wait… look!
[0:21.1] Pip IS flying… underwater!
[0:23.3] Penguins use their wings to fly through the sea!
[0:27.9] Can YOU flap like Pip?
```

Pip's own sounds ("wheee!", "ah… ACHOO!", a giggle on the wink) go in during the edit; they are not generated in the clips.

## Music & sound

- **Music:** playful ukulele + marimba + light hand percussion, instrumental, **120 BPM**. At 120 BPM a beat is 0.5 s, and every cut in the beat sheet falls on a multiple of 0.5 s, so every cut lands on a beat. 30 s is exactly 15 bars, which loops cleanly.
- Duck the music under the VO; let it swell on the twist (0:21).
- **SFX:** one cue per motion, from the table above — percussive and cartoony, never scary. The splash (0:18) and the rocket whoosh (0:21) are the two biggest hits.

## Loop

- Shot 13 is generated with shot 01's keyframe as its **end frame**, so the last frame of the Short is the first.
- Cut the music on the bar line — no fade-out tail.
- No end card or "subscribe" screen; either one breaks the loop. The last VO line is the call to action.

## First frame / thumbnail

Shot 01's keyframe: one character, big eyes, red scarf against blue sky — readable at thumbnail size.

## Kid-safe checklist

- Slapstick only: Pip is never hurt, and never scared for more than a beat.
- Bright water, no dark depths, no predators, no flashing or strobing.
- The balloon strings stay in Pip's flipper — never around his neck.
- Fact: ✅ penguins can't fly in the air; their wings are stiff flippers that they beat to "fly" through the water.
