# Fact-check & conversions: كيف تطوّر الخداع عبر العصور

Every factual claim in the VO is graded below.

- ✅ Solid: well documented. Say it as written.
- ⚠️ Soft: a legend, an ancient figure, an estimate, a live market price, or a rhetorical point. The VO already hedges it («يقولون»، «أحياناً»، «تقريباً»، «ممكن»), so keep the hedge when you record.
- 🧮 Conversion: the arithmetic is in [Conversion math](#conversion-math).

Checked on **30 Sep 2026**. Anything tied to a market price moves, so re-check the list at the bottom before recording the VO.

## Conversion math

| Item | Inputs | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Themistocles' bounty (#17) | 200 talents × ~26 kg = 5,200 kg = 167,183 troy oz × ~$62 | **≈ $10.4M** |
| Weight in gold (#28) | 1 kg = 32.15 oz × $4,182 | **≈ $134,450** |
| Armada (#36) | 10M ducats × ~3.545 g = 35.45 t = 1.14M oz × $4,182 | **≈ $4.77B** |
| Garbo's pay (#52) | $340,000 × (CPI 2025 ≈ 322 ÷ CPI 1944 ≈ 17.6 ≈ 18.3) | **≈ $6.2M** |
| A day of the US military (#9) | $954B ÷ 365 | **≈ $2.6B/day**; a tenth ≈ **$260M** |

Prices used: gold **$4,182.15/oz** (29 Sep 2026); silver **~$62/oz** (end of Sep 2026).

## Re-check before recording

- **Silver** (#17): the ~$10M figure moves with the silver price.
- **Gold** (#28, #36): the $134k and $4.7B figures move with the gold price.
- **SIPRI** (#9): the next release is in late April 2027 and will replace the $954 bn US figure.
- **US intelligence budget** (#61): the FY2026 NIP/MIP totals are published after the fiscal year closes. "Over $100 billion" is very likely to hold.
