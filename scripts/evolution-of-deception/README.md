**Title:** كيف تطوّر الخداع عبر العصور… وأخطر مخادع اليوم في جيبك
**Runtime:** ~10:01 (1,416 VO words at 150 WPM, plus ~4 s of transition per section)
**Thesis (hook and close):** the most dangerous deception isn't the cleverest lie; it's the one the victim already *wants* to believe. The deceiver supplies the story and the victim finishes it, sometimes without a single lie being told.
**Payoff:** the hook promises "the most dangerous deceiver in history, in your pocket right now, that has never told you a lie". The finale goes from deepfake heists ($35M in the UAE, $25M in Hong Kong) to the recommendation feed.
**Through-line:** intelligence is cheap and force is expensive. Sun Tzu's "a tenth of one day's war", Walsingham's £2,000 against the Armada's 10M ducats, and a piece of wood against a $100B intelligence budget.

Every chapter is 152–179 words and runs 65–76 s, inside the framework's 130–180 words and 60–90 s. All eight dialect markers (تخيلوا، لاعب بالفلوس لعب، طفرانيين، حوسة، شقة، شايل كاش، نقطة على السطر، الكشخة) and all three bridges (وعلى طاري، لكن القصة مو كلها وردية، وما وقف هنا) appear in the VO.

## Recurring visual system

- **The Mirror**: an ornate mirror that shows each victim what they want to see. Xerxes sees fleeing ships, the courier bows to Walsingham, the ambassador sees "friendship". At the close it becomes a phone screen reflecting the viewer.
- **Timeline ribbon**: a thin bar along the bottom showing the current year across 2,500 years.

## Production notes

- **Keep violence implied.** Mary's execution, al-Kindi's beating and the Salamis battle are all implied, never shown.
- **Hitler appears only as a silhouette**, with no face and no symbols beyond what history requires.
- **No real logos or trademarks** in visuals: Wordle, WhatsApp, Snapchat, the *300* poster, FIFA and bank-card brands are referenced in the VO only, with generic designs on screen.
- **Legends are flagged in the VO**: «يقولون» (the weight-in-gold story) and the open question of whether Sun Tzu existed. Keep those hedges when recording.
- **Timing assumes 150 WPM.** Record a scratch VO first and re-time the cuts to your actual read.
