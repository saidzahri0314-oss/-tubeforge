# Fact-check & conversions: أخطر جيوش التاريخ

Every factual claim in the VO is graded below.

- ✅ Solid: well documented. Say it as written.
- ⚠️ Soft: an ancient figure, a legend, an estimate, or a live market price. The VO already hedges it («يقولون»، «حسب الرواية»، «تقريباً»), so keep the hedge when you record.
- 🧮 Conversion: the arithmetic is in [Conversion math](#conversion-math).

Checked on **30 Sep 2026**. Anything tied to a market price moves, so re-check the list at the bottom before recording the VO.

| 10 | Sennacherib was killed by his sons inside a temple | ✅ | Arda-Mulissu and Nabu-shar-usur, Nineveh, 20 Oct 681 BC. The Bible (2 Kings 19:37) agrees on sons and a temple. |
| 11 | Nineveh fell less than 70 years later | ✅ | 612 BC, 69 years after 681 BC. |
| 12 | A Greek army passed the ruins about two centuries later and didn't know the city's name | ✅ | Xenophon, *Anabasis* 3.4.7–12 (401 BC). He calls Nimrud "Larisa" and Nineveh "Mespila" and attributes both to the Medes. |
| 13 | "I shut him up in Jerusalem… like a bird in a cage" | ✅ | Sennacherib's annals (Taylor / Oriental Institute prisms). The prisms are clay, so the VO says «بالخط المسماري», not "carved in stone". |

## Conversion math

| Item | Inputs | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Hezekiah's gold (#9) | 30 talents × ~30 kg = 900 kg = 28,936 troy oz × $4,182 | **≈ $121M**. "Over $100M" holds while gold is above ~$3,460/oz. |
| Byzantine pawn (#38) | 30,000 ducats × 3.545 g = 106.35 kg = 3,419 oz × $4,182 | **≈ $14.3M** |
| Persian treasure (#19) | 180,000 talents × ~26 kg = 4,680 t = 150.5M oz × ~$62 | **≈ $9.3B** |
| Share of world silver (#19) | 4,680 t ÷ (846.6M oz × 31.1035 g = 26,332 t) | **≈ 17.8%**, "close to a fifth" |
| Mongol empire (#33) | 24M km² ÷ 148.9M km² land; 24M ÷ 2.15M km² | **16.1%** of land; **11.2×** Saudi Arabia |
| Louisiana (#45) | 2.144M km² vs 2.150M km² | **99.7%** of Saudi Arabia's area |
| Drone exchange rate (#60) | $4,500,000 ÷ $500 (or ÷ $400) | **9,000–11,250 : 1** |

Prices used: gold **$4,182.15/oz** (29 Sep 2026); silver **~$62/oz** (end of Sep 2026 close $62.17, with a September range of $57.86–$68.98).

## Re-check before recording

- **Gold** (#9, #38): the $14M figure moves with the gold price. "Over $100M" is safe above ~$3,460/oz.
- **Silver** (#19): the "~$9B" figure moves with the silver price. If silver is outside roughly $55–$70, update the number.
- **SIPRI** (#59): the next release is in late April 2027. Update the year and total if you publish after it.
- **Saudi budget** (#27): the 2027 budget is announced around December 2026. "Over $300B" will very likely still hold.
