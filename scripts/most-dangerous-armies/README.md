**Title:** أخطر جيوش التاريخ… وليش أخطرها ممكن يكلّف 500 دولار بس؟
**Runtime:** ~10:05 (1,426 VO words at 150 WPM, plus ~4 s of transition per section)
**Thesis (hook and close):** an army is a machine: money goes in, fear comes out. The most dangerous army isn't the biggest or the richest; it's the one with the best «سعر صرف», the most fear for the least money.
**Payoff:** the hook promises an army that breaks the equation. The finale delivers a ~$500 drone against a $4.5M tank, and Stuxnet, which did its damage with no soldiers.

Every chapter is 149–176 words and runs 63–74 s, inside the framework's 130–180 words and 60–90 s. All eight dialect markers (تخيلوا، لاعب بالفلوس لعب، طفرانيين، حوسة، شقة، شايل كاش، نقطة على السطر، الكشخة) and all three bridges (وعلى طاري، لكن القصة مو كلها وردية، وما وقف هنا) appear in the VO.

## Recurring visual system

- **The Fear Machine**: a 3D brass-and-iron contraption. Coins go in and black smoke shaped like soldiers comes out. It opens the video, and at the close it shrinks to drone size.
- **سعر صرف الرعب gauge**: a persistent corner UI that updates at the end of every era and snaps off its axis at the drone.
- **Timeline ribbon**: a thin bar along the bottom showing the current year, which keeps viewers oriented across 2,700 years.

## Production notes

- **Keep violence implied.** Cut away before impalement, flaying or execution, and use abstract white flashes for the bomb. This keeps the video advertiser-friendly and the script already implies rather than describes.
- **No real logos or trademarks** in visuals: Amazon, Xbox, Minecraft and Barbie are referenced in the VO only, with generic designs on screen.
- **Portray Genghis Khan and Mehmed II respectfully.** Mehmed's "child star" beat is a comeback story, not a joke at his expense.
- **Legends are flagged in the VO**: «يقولون» (Crassus's gold) and «حسب الرواية» (Alexander's «للأقوى»). Keep those hedges when recording.
- **Timing assumes 150 WPM.** Record a scratch VO first and re-time the cuts to your actual read.
